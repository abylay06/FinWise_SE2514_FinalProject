Frequently asked questions

How do I start investing with FinWise?

Explore Investment Planning to see what a first conversation might cover. You can try a consultation request to practice describing your goals.

Where does FinWise keep my information?

This is a student project. Demo account information and saved activities stay in your browser. Use sample details when trying the forms.

Can I change my retirement plan later?

Yes. When your goals or time frame change, you can revisit the questions in Retirement Planning and save a new consultation request.
